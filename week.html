My week

Put dinners on the days you will cook them. Each day shows its dishes together as one meal, with a suggested pairing. Order them by freshness if you like, then add the week to your calendar, with the recipe card inside each event. Everything stays in this browser.

Freshness

Fresh fish, ground meat and chicken keep only 1 to 2 days in the fridge; steaks and ribs 3 to 5; most vegetables longer. Order by freshness suggests cooking the shortest-lived meals first. It only suggests: you choose, and pinned days never move.

Assumes you buy fresh at the start of the week; freezing extends it. Numbers are conservative USDA refrigerator ranges (a few are marked as estimates). Package dates and your own judgement come first. Sources are listed in data/freshness.js.

The .ics file has one event per planned day. The event's description is the recipe card: dishes, servings, ingredients (scaled to your servings, with your swaps), numbered steps, the pairing line and recipe links. Each day also has an Add to Google Calendar link (its card is shortened to fit a link; the recipe links are kept). We have checked the .ics with a standards validator. We have not yet tried it in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook, so tell us if an import looks off. Links in the card point to this site as it is hosted today. "Shop this week" replaces the recipes on your shopping list with this week's, at the servings you planned.

Move this week to another device

The share link carries the plan inside the link itself, so nothing is uploaded. Opening it on another device asks before replacing that device's week.
