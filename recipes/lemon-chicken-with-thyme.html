Lemon Chicken With Thyme

Adapted from Extra Light Cookie Flyer

Serves 4

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Combine the flour, salt and pepper in a shallow bowl.
  2. Dredge the chicken breasts in the flour to coat completely. Reserve the leftover flour.
  3. Warm 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. Add the chicken and brown on one side, about 5 minutes.
  5. Add the remaining oil, turn the chicken, and brown well on the other side, about 5 minutes longer.
  6. Transfer the chicken to a plate and set aside.
  7. Add the butter to the skillet. When it melts, add the onions and cook, stirring, until softened, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  8. Stir in the reserved seasoned flour and cook, stirring, until completely incorporated, about 1 minute.
  9. Add the broth, 3 tablespoons lemon juice and the thyme.
  10. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly.
  11. Return the chicken to the skillet, reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook until the chicken is tender, about 30 minutes.

Good to know

  • The original steps don't say where the parsley goes; sprinkling it over the finished dish is a common choice.
