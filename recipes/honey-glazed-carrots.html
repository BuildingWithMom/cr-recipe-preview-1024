Honey-Glazed Carrots

Adapted from Cooking Light (December 2005)

Serves 8

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a medium saucepan.
  2. Add the carrots and cook for 20 minutes or until tender.
  3. Drain well.
  4. Place the carrots and the remaining ingredients in a large bowl and toss gently.
