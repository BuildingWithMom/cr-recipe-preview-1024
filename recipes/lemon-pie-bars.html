Lemon Pie Bars

Adapted from Allrecipes.com

Serves 24

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix 2 cups of the flour and the confectioner's sugar together.
  3. Cut in the butter and mix well until the dough resembles pie dough.
  4. Press the dough into a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  5. Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until golden brown.
  6. Beat together the eggs, granulated sugar, 4 tablespoons flour, lemon juice and lemon zest for 2 minutes to combine fully.
  7. Pour over the baked crust.
  8. Bake another 18 to 20 minutes, or until the lemon topping is set.
  9. Sprinkle with confectioner's sugar when cooled.
