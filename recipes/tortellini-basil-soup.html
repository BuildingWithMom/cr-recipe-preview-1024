Tortellini-Basil Soup

Adapted from Cooking Light

Serves 7

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Ingredients

Tick "I have it" for items already in your kitchen. They'll be left off your list.

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a boil in a large Dutch oven.
  2. Add the tortellini and cook 6 minutes or until tender.
  3. Stir in the beans and tomatoes.
  4. Reduce the heat and simmer 5 minutes, or until heated through.
  5. Remove from the heat and stir in the basil, vinegar and salt.

Good to know

  • Don't overcook the tortellini.
  • The original steps don't say when to add the Parmesan and pepper.
