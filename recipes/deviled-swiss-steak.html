Deviled Swiss Steak

Family recipe

Serves 8

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Sauté the onion until soft. Remove and set aside.
  2. Combine the flour, salt and pepper.
  3. Pound the flour mixture into the steak (or dredge the steak in it).
  4. Brown the meat.
  5. Add the tomatoes, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, hot pepper sauce, garlic powder, lemon juice, brown sugar and the onions.
  6. Cover and simmer for 2 1/2 hours.
