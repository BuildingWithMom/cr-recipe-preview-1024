Rice and Noodle Pilaf

Adapted from Eating Well, Rush Hour Cookbook

Serves 4

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the noodles and cook, stirring constantly, until golden brown, 3 to 5 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the broth and bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce the heat to low and simmer, covered, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice and noodles are tender, about 20 minutes.
  6. Remove from the heat and stir in the parsley. Season with salt and pepper.
