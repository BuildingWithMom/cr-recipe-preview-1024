Barbecued Spare Ribs

Family recipe

Serves 8

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Ingredients

  • Barbecue Sauce

Tick "I have it" for items already in your kitchen. They move to the bottom and are left off your list.

Method

  1. In a large pot, cover the ribs with water, add salt to taste, and simmer over medium-low heat for 1 hour.
  2. Meanwhile, make the sauce by combining all the sauce ingredients.
  3. Drain the ribs and place them in a covered dish.
  4. Pour the sauce over the ribs, coating each one.
  5. Let cool, cover, and refrigerate overnight.
  6. Grill over hot coals or broil until browned and heated through, about 20 minutes, brushing with sauce as needed.
