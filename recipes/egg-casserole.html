Breakfast

Egg Casserole

Family recipe

Serves 10

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Ingredients

Tick "I have it" for items already in your kitchen. They move to the bottom and are left off your list.

Method

  1. In the bottom of a greased 9x13-inch glass baking dish, combine the croutons and cheese.
  2. Lightly beat the eggs, milk, onion powder and pepper.
  3. Pour over the crouton and cheese mixture.
  4. Refrigerate overnight.
  5. Before baking, crumble the bacon on top.
  6. Bake at 325°F for 50 to 60 minutes.

Good to know

  • For a thicker casserole, double the recipe and bake for an hour or a little more.
