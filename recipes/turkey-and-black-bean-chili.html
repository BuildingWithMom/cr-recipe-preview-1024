Turkey and Black Bean Chili

Adapted from Cooking Light (1997)

Serves 5

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Ingredients

Tick "I have it" for items already in your kitchen. They'll be left off your list.

Method

  1. Cook the turkey in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat until browned, stirring to crumble.
  2. Drain in a colander.
  3. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium heat.
  4. Add the garlic and onion and sauté for 7 minutes.
  5. Add the chili powder, oregano, cumin, salt and red pepper. Cook 1 minute.
  6. Return the turkey to the skillet and add the water, prunes and beans.
  7. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer uncovered for 5 minutes.
  8. Serve over rice if desired.

Good to know

  • Rice for serving isn't in the ingredient list.
