Pulled Chicken Sandwiches

Adapted from Better Homes and Gardens

Serves 12

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Pull the meat from the chickens, discarding the skin and bones. Use two forks to shred the meat.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat.
  3. Add the onions and red pepper and cook, stirring, about 5 minutes or until tender.
  4. Add the vinegar, cook 1 minute, and stir.
  5. Stir in the tomato sauce, basil, honey and salt.
  6. Bring to a boil.
  7. Add the chicken and toss gently. Heat through.
  8. To serve, use a slotted spoon to place the chicken mixture on split kaiser rolls.
  9. Top with bread-and-butter pickle slices.

Good to know

  • Kaiser rolls and bread-and-butter pickles for serving aren't in the ingredient list.
