Beef Barley Soup

Family recipe

Serves 10

Only ingredient amounts change. Adjust pan size and cooking times yourself when scaling up or down.

Method

  1. Brown the short ribs in a little oil.
  2. Add the water, tomatoes, onion and salt. Bring to a boil.
  3. Cover and simmer for 1 1/2 hours.
  4. Add the remaining ingredients.
  5. Cover and simmer for an additional 45 minutes.
  6. Remove the meat from the bones and heat through.
  7. Skim off the excess fat.

Good to know

  • The original steps use onion and a little oil but don't list an amount for either.
  • Optional add-ins: 3/4 cup chopped green pepper, 1 medium chopped potato, 1 wedge of cabbage, peas, corn or green beans.
